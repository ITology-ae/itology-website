Mohamed Abdul Rahim

Profile

Experience & Expertise

01

As CEO at OSC4i, Mohamed leads the development of governance strategies that strengthen organizational resilience, reinforce executive decision-making, and align corporate objectives with effective governance, enterprise risk management, and operational excellence. His leadership philosophy is founded on the belief that governance is not merely a compliance obligation but a strategic capability that enables sustainable performance, innovation, stakeholder confidence, and long-term value creation.

02

Throughout his career, Mohamed has advised senior government leaders, executive management teams, and boards on governance transformation, enterprise risk management, cybersecurity, business architecture, and organizational strategy. He has successfully designed and implemented governance operating models, integrated enterprise risk management frameworks, and strategic oversight mechanisms that enhance accountability, accelerate decision-making, and support organizational agility in increasingly complex business environments.

03

His expertise spans corporate governance, board advisory, enterprise risk management, cybersecurity governance, strategic planning, digital transformation, business continuity, crisis management, and organizational performance. Mohamed has led complex transformation programs for governments and multinational organizations, delivering governance frameworks that integrate strategy, risk, technology, and operational execution into a unified management approach that supports sustainable growth and resilience.

04

Mohamed has played a significant role in advancing governance and cybersecurity capabilities at both organizational and national levels. His contributions include supporting the development of the Jordanian National Cybersecurity Framework, leading enterprise transformation initiatives across the UAE, and advising organizations on strengthening governance structures, information security, and business continuity. His work has consistently focused on enabling organizations to navigate emerging risks while capitalizing on opportunities created by digital innovation.

05

Recognized internationally as a trusted advisor and thought leader, Mohamed serves as an Advisory Board Member of the FAIR Institute, Co-Chair of the FAIR Institute Jordan Chapter, and Vice President of the DAMA Jordan Chapter. These leadership roles reflect his commitment to advancing governance excellence, risk-informed decision-making, and the adoption of globally recognized best practices in cybersecurity, information management, and enterprise resilience.

06

At OSC4i, Mohamed continues to champion governance as a strategic enabler of business success, empowering organizations to strengthen oversight, enhance transparency, manage enterprise risks proactively, and build trusted, future-ready institutions capable of thriving in a rapidly evolving digital economy.
